About Kedim Blocks

Kedim Blocks is a calm block puzzle with Kedim, the orange cat. Drag the three clay blocks from the bottom onto the 8 by 8 board. Fill a whole row or column and it pops away with a happy meow.

Pop lines again and again for a combo and big points. The game ends when none of your blocks fit, so leave room for the big ones. Play Endless for your best score, try the new Daily picture board every day, or go on a 40 level Adventure where you pop lines to collect fish. Earn Batik and Kuih block skins just by playing.

3 quick tips

  1. Keep the middle of the board open: big 3 by 3 blocks need space.
  2. Look at all three blocks before you place the first one.
  3. Pop a line every few moves to keep your combo going for bigger points.
